## Session hygiene

When a milestone lands in this session (a feature shipped and pushed, a
deploy stabilized, a round of debugging resolved, docs updated to reflect
it) — say so explicitly and suggest starting a fresh session for the next
piece of work, rather than continuing to accumulate unrelated context.
Point to `DECISIONS.md` (why) and `NOTES.md` (what's open) as what a new
session should read first; don't re-explain them inline. See "Current
status" in `README.md` and the session-strategy discussion in this
project's history for the full reasoning.
